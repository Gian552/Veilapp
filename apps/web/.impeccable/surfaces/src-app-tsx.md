# Surface brief: VEIL app (all screens)

Scope: the whole web app (onboarding, sign-in, chat list and conversation, verify panel, settings, Panic Reset). Mode: operate.
User answers (2026-10-25): everything about the old look bothered them (pattern avatars, colours, empty/generic, too much text); wants an own identity; no playful/colourful, no hacker/crypto look, dark stays default. Chose direction A "Numbered Account" (Tresor).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your identity is a number engraved on a brass plate; everything else stays locked in the vault. Refuses the grey Signal clone and the neon-terminal "privacy" costume.

OWN-WORLD: One tonal ramp of vault green (deep bottle green grounds, leather-dark rail), parchment text, brass reserved for exactly two jobs: your own identity plate and the active selection/primary action. Oxblood only for danger, verdigris only for "verified". Contacts are monogram plates (two initials of the two words, engraved serif); names set in an engraved serif, UI in a quiet grotesk. Hairline-ruled lists, no padded floating cards.

STORY: The person sees at once who they are (their plate), who they talk to, and that nothing else is kept. Verifying a contact feels like checking an account number. Dangerous acts (Panic Reset, key change) are calm and deliberate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail (leather, 330px): wordmark, your brass plate, new-conversation field with the existence hint, ruled conversation plates. Centre: conversation header with plate and verification state, messages, composer. Right (on demand): engraved safety-number plate with the primary "Numbers match" action.

FORM: Numbered Account (Swiss numbered accounts and safe-deposit vaults), candidate 4 of the ordered list. seed:88be026404e902e2

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
